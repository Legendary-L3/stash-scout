Connect Google Gemini

Stash Scout uses Google's Gemini AI to recognize items and search the web for prices. You need a free API key. It is saved only on this phone.

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key, then copy it.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save.

      Add Stash Scout to your Home Screen

      It opens full screen like an app, and Safari keeps your collection safe. Websites that are not on the Home Screen can have their saved data cleared after a few weeks of not being used.

      1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
      2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

      Gemini API key

      Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. Turning on billing for the key stops that.
